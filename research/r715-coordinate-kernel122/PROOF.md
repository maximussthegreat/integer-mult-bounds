## 1. A coordinate-only response kernel

For each helper h, define its response as the F2 incidence vector of targets whose initial D compensation reads h. The witness `best_kernel_selection.json` contains 100 relations

`response(pivot) = XOR response(donor)`.

Each participant is a non-retained helper. Pivots never serve as donors; each donor serves one entrance E. E is contained in every participant’s first nonzero rational frame. The 78 rank-one and 22 rank-two pivots have total entrance rank 122.

For every relation, start the pivot at E, omit its D compensation, inject the pivot into each donor at E before A, and undo that injection at the full frame after C. Exact replay verifies all 955 independent F2 columns, including helper dirt, are restored except the intended `y_t += x_t`. Rational frame nesting and integer source-support constraints were checked for the raw and kernel words.

All entrance root indices lie in 0 through 7, and the committed root vectors at these indices are precisely twice the standard coordinate vectors. Thus E is a literal coordinate subspace. Its orthogonal complement is mapped to a standard coordinate slot by a coordinate permutation, preserving the ambient form I8/2 and the slot form I_(8-dim E)/2. `isometry_certificate.json` lists all 12 distinct nonzero entrance maps. No new denominator primes are introduced.

### Rejected predecessor

The exploratory rank-123 witness included E spanned by roots `[1,7,10,11,12,13]`. Its Euclidean determinant has squareclass 2, so it cannot be identified with the standard rank-three coordinate subspace required by this hosting interface. It is **rejected**. It was replaced by its rank-two coordinate subspace spanned by roots `[1,7]`. No final profile, bound or constant uses the rank-123 version.

## 2. Target mixing, including chains

The frozen witness has 84 receivers and 28 receiver-as-donor links, with 28 mixing frames of dimension 5 and 56 of dimension 6. Every receiver/donor pair has identical original delivered B prefixes inside its common frame, and later receiver frames contain that frame.

Preconditioning is in **reverse topological order**: `y_u += y_donor` must use the donor’s original initial value. The scatter correction likewise uses the original donor row, rather than a previously transformed row. B-phase target additions are in forward topological order, after the relevant helper deliveries. The resulting word passes the complete955-column F2 replay and exact nested-frame checks. A forward-topological D preconditioner is independently rejected by a negative control.

The exact per-call paid profile is

There are 15,322 local ADDs. Residual widths are `{6:22, 7:78, 8:615}`.

## 4. New finite charges

No previous coefficient invoice is reused. Initialize a nonnegative L1 envelope of1 for every physical family and0 for the private stream. Traverse all 857,296 expanded local events and every signed bridge shear. Each literal ADD updates the actual destination owner by `B_dst += |c| B_src`, including collisions of logical roles on one family. COPY transfers the bound; ERASE resets the private stream. Coordinate/address permutations do not enlarge the value envelope.

This gives a 607-bit **scalar physical-family** maximum, conservatively charged 640 bits. It is **not a certified complete routed/literal compiler-prefix bound**. Within the inherited conditional bill categories, the newly evaluated finite coefficient has 607 bits; the three-level quantity `(coefficient+1)^3*(paid_children+1)^3` has 1874 bits, charged 1920. The invoice uses the pinned operation categories with deliberately generous finite coordinate-elimination charges, at most `2*m^2` factors for each of the two route layers. All actual new residual maps are merely permutations.

The elementary orthogonal-cover stock exponent gives a complete row coefficient 19428, below the retained 20161 allowance. All six bootstrap cutoffs use the new 1920-bit charge and satisfy their exact inequalities.

This is a fresh conservative component bill within the inherited compiler categorization. It is not an independent proof that the entire all-size compiler or every inherited bill category is adequate.

## 5. Exact arithmetic and controls

`certify_moments.py` uses Decimal only to suggest a grid interval. Both reported endpoint signs are proved with exact rational bounds:100-term atanh series for logarithms,40-term exponential series, and explicit positive remainder bounds. The sharp bad-class term is

`(146478 * 2 * 78^3 * number_of_children)/(2^80 * 78 * W) * 78^c`.

The first endpoint’s moment is strictly below 1; the adjacent 1e-24 endpoint’s moment is strictly above 1. Six exact bootstrap steps, all 47 strict balanced-assembly inequalities, and rejection of the next 1e-18 κ grid point for this fixed six-bootstrap parameter choice are recorded in `moment_certificate.json`.

`controls_certificate.json` records 35 rejected mutations: five kernel/mixing corruptions, six ownership/slot corruptions, and 24 omitted bridge shears. A separate reviewer independently reconstructed the repaired kernel, mixing, ownership, physical scalar envelope, exact moment endpoints and outer arithmetic; its report is supplied separately. The reviewer did not import these checkers.
